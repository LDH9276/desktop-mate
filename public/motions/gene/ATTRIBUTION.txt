Runtime adaptations in Mate:
- Six added clips retain torso, shoulders, arms, elbows, wrists and fingers.
  This includes thumb and distal finger tracks; Mate also adds relaxed finger
  curls and per-joint follow-through when transitioning between poses.
  Source rest-bone reference frames are derived from Gene_light.pmx and stored
  in src/gene-rest.json, then retargeted to the selected VRM/PMX character.
  The Gene model itself is not bundled.
- Clips are excerpted, retimed to short reactions and crossfaded to neutral.
  Original Bezier rotation interpolation is retained.
- Apology and sleepy use only torso, neck and head rotations; apology uses
  its first 90 frames. The other limbs use Mate's own animation.
- Original face/accessory tracks and root translations are not applied.
  Mate adds facial expressions, reaction anticipation/accents, body turns,
  asymmetric leg poses, stomps, laughter pulses and foot-height correction.
- These are edited character reactions, not verbatim playback of the source.
No endorsement by the original creators is implied.
